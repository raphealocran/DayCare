What sets us apart

Everything a growing child needs, under one roof.

Certified Teachers

Every educator holds an ECE credential and completes annual background checks. Low turnover means familiar faces your child trusts.

Safe Licensed

State-licensed with monthly inspections and a 4:1 child-to-teacher ratio for infants, 8:1 for preschoolers. Safety is never an afterthought.

Structured Curriculum

A Reggio Emilia-inspired, play-based approach that follows each child's curiosity while building kindergarten readiness.

Nutritious Meals

Chef-prepared breakfast, lunch, and snacks made with locally sourced ingredients. Allergy-friendly options always available.

Creative Arts

Daily painting, music, and dramatic play. We celebrate the mess because it's where imagination takes root.

Outdoor Play

Two secure play yards, a vegetable garden, and a splash pad. Fresh air and gross-motor play are part of every day.
